Family-Owned Electrical Workshop in Savusavu, Fiji

We are a family-owned electrical workshop proudly serving Savusavu and the surrounding communities of Vanua Levu, Fiji. With years of hands-on experience and a strong commitment to quality workmanship, we provide reliable electrical solutions for domestic, automotive, marine, industrial, and commercial needs.

Our services include commercial electrical installation, marine electrical work, motor winding, auto electrical repairs, electrical appliance repairs, and generator servicing. We work with a wide range of equipment and are dedicated to finding practical, dependable solutions for every customer.

As a local family-owned business, we take pride in providing honest service, quality workmanship, and personalized attention to our customers. Whether it’s a small electrical repair or a larger industrial or marine job, our goal is to keep your equipment and electrical systems working safely and reliably.
